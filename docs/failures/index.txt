<h2 id="attribution"><a class="anchor" href="#attribution" aria-label="Link to this section">Attribution</a></h2><p><code>failedStage</code> is the most recent stage recorded when the throw happened. That is why stages are
worth marking even when nothing consumes them individually: they are what turns &quot;this flow failed&quot;
into &quot;this flow failed at the payment gateway call&quot;.</p>
<p>If no stage was recorded, <code>failedStage</code> is absent and the message is just
<code>flow &lt;label&gt; failed</code>.</p>
<p>Attribution survives the <a href="/docs/safety/">stage cap</a> — the last stage name is tracked separately from
the retained list, so a flow that dropped 40 000 middle stages still names the one it died on.</p>
<h2 id="non-error-throws"><a class="anchor" href="#non-error-throws" aria-label="Link to this section">Non-Error throws</a></h2><p>Anything can be thrown in JavaScript. A non-<code>Error</code> is recorded without pretending otherwise:</p>

decide per error:</p>

The operation
failed, but nothing propagates out for <code>run()</code> to see. Tell chiplog:</p>
<div class="code-block"><pre class="shiki shiki-themes light-plus dark-plus" style="--shiki-light:#000000;--shiki-dark:#D4D4D4;--shiki-light-bg:#FFFFFF;--shiki-dark-bg:#1E1E1E" tabindex="0"><code><span class="line"><span style="--shiki-light:#001080;--shiki-dark:#9CDCFE">flow</span><span style="--shiki-light:#000000;--shiki-dark:#D4D4D4">.</span><span style="--shiki-light:#795E26;--shiki-dark:#DCDCAA">fail</span><span style="--shiki-light:#000000;--shiki-dark:#D4D4D4">(</span><span style="--shiki-light:#001080;--shiki-dark:#9CDCFE">error</span><span style="--shiki-light:#000000;--shiki-dark:#D4D4D4">);</span></span></code></pre></div><p>Both bundled adapters do exactly this — see <a href="/docs/adapters/">Adapters</a>.</p>
<h2 id="what-counts-as-a-failure"><a class="anchor" href="#what-counts-as-a-failure" aria-label="Link to this section">What counts as a failure</a></h2><p><code>fail()</code> is for a <em>failure of your service</em>. A 404, a rejected password, a validation error — those
are answers, and recording them as failed flows drowns the signal that the failed path exists for.
They are already visible as <code>status</code> on the event.</p>
<p>The Elysia adapter takes that position by default: routing and validation outcomes are <code>ok</code> with
their status attached. It is one option away if you disagree.</p>
<div class="callout callout-gap"><p class="callout-label">Warning</p><p>A throwing sink is caught and never rethrown. Flushing happens in a <code>finally</code>, so letting a
logging failure escape would replace the error your caller is already handling. Pass <code>onSinkError</code>
if you want to know about it.</p>
