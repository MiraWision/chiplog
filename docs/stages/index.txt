<p>Outside any flow, <code>stage()</code> and <code>set()</code> are silent no-ops. Logging must never be the thing that
throws, and a helper called from both a flow and a script should not need to know which.</p>

handle and the ambient <code>stage()</code> where you do not; they are the same flow.</p>
